## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Elite / Super Complex** | Doctoral/post-doctoral training, life-or-death consequences, years of residency or high-stakes apprenticeship |
| **Expert / Very Complex** | Graduate degree or multi-year licensure; error carries serious regulatory/safety consequences |
| **Advanced** | 2–6 years of formal apprenticeship or degree; state licensing typically required |
| **Skilled** | 1–4 years of honing; certification/licensure typically required; competence is demonstrable and tested |
| **Semi-Skilled** | Roughly 1 year of on-the-job or vocational training; some certification expected |
| **Low-Skilled** | Minimal formal training; proficiency gained within weeks to a few months |
| **Unskilled** | No credential required; task mastery achievable in days to weeks |
